## Welcome

ROOTED is a chapter-by-chapter commentary on the entire New Testament, from Matthew 1 to Revelation 22. It is written for readers who want to understand Scripture deeply but may not have formal theological training. The goal is to walk through each chapter the way a patient Bible teacher would: explaining what is happening, who is speaking and why, what the words meant to their first hearers, and how the teaching applies to life today.

## How Each Chapter Is Organized

Every chapter study follows the same pattern. It begins with a **Chapter Theme** and a short summary of the **Main Teaching**. The **Chapter Background and Story** then walks through the whole chapter in order, explaining people, places, customs, and the flow of the narrative or argument. Under **Important Verses Explained**, two to four key verses are studied in depth, each with a Simple Explanation, a Deeper Spiritual Meaning, and a section on Applying This Teaching to Life Today. A wider section, **How to Apply This Chapter to Life Today**, shows how the chapter's teachings work together in everyday life. Each chapter closes with a single **Chapter Prayer**.

## About the Scripture Quotations

Scripture quotations are from the World English Bible (WEB), a modern English translation in the public domain. Every key verse and every quoted phrase in this book was checked word for word against the WEB text published by eBible.org. Quotations from Scripture appear in italics, and references are italicized as well. Short words and phrases are sometimes quoted from within a longer verse; the full verse can be read through the link at the top of each chapter. Some verses mentioned in the commentary are absent from many early manuscripts, and where that matters, the commentary says so.

## About Interpretation

The commentary tries to explain what each passage actually says in its own setting before drawing out its spiritual meaning. Where faithful Christians have understood a passage in different ways, such as on the Lord's Supper, baptism, church leadership, divorce and remarriage, or the details of end-times prophecy, the main views are described briefly and fairly rather than presented as settled. Passages about suffering, submission, forgiveness, and marriage are never meant to require anyone to remain in danger or to tolerate abuse; seeking safety, protection, and help is consistent with faithfulness to Christ.

## About the Cover

This edition keeps the original ROOTED cover artwork. The cover's reference to the Navigators 5x5x5 method comes from an earlier workbook edition; this edition is a reading commentary rather than a workbook, so it contains explanations and applications instead of exercises, quizzes, or review schedules.
